SysML v2 in a Digital Engineering Workflow: From deployed SysML v1 models to validated, executable digital artifacts

Abstract: 

This talk presents a practical digital-engineering workflow for extending deployed SysML v1 and Cameo models into a standards-based SysML v2 ecosystem. The approach combines model extraction, clean-room transformation, semantic validation, live Cameo integration, and connections to analysis, simulation, visualization, and automation tools. Using work associated with past architecture efforts, we show how textual SysML v2 can support version control, reproducible transformations, automated checking, and broader model utilization without abandoning existing configuration-management practices. The central premise is a governed, closed-loop digital thread: preserve engineering intent during transformation, use the resulting model to generate executable artifacts and analytical evidence, and feed results back into controlled model evolution with traceability.

Speaker Bio:

Dr. Jon R. Fox is Division Chief Scientist of the Architecture and System Development Division in the Applied Systems Laboratory at the Georgia Tech Research Institute (GTRI) in Huntsville, Alabama, where he provides technical direction, review, and mentoring across division programs in model-based systems engineering, architecture, and system development.

An applied physicist with 25 years of post-doctoral experience, he holds a Ph.D. in experimental condensed-matter physics from Penn State and has worked across MEMS device design and fabrication, multiphysics modeling, signal processing, and digital engineering, from SBIR-funded microfabrication research to SysML modeling of rotorcraft, railgun, and laser effect systems.

Dr. Fox serves as GTRI Technical Lead for Future Long-Range Assault Aircraft (FLRAA) architectural risk reduction under the Future Vertical Lift Architectural Framework. He is an active open-source developer and maintainer of sysmlpy, a clean-room Python toolchain for the OMG SysML v2 standard validated through OMG conformance testing, along with uml2py and sysml_canvas, and curates the awesome-sysml list of SysML v2 language tools. He is an INCOSE Certified Systems Engineering Professional (CSEP). His shared code lives at github.com/mycr0ft.
